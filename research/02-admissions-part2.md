## 8. IMPLICATIONS FOR MARIA (brief notes)

- With ~1 yr experience at Oct 2026 (started late 2025), she will have roughly 2.3-2.5 yrs by March 2028 and ~3 yrs by late 2028. ISB PGP's 24-month rule (as of March 31) is likely met for the 2028-29 intake; the exact reference year for "as of March 31st" was not stated on the page, so confirm on isb.edu.
