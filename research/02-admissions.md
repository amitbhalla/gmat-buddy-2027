### 1.2 ISB PGP (flagship, Hyderabad and Mohali)

**Eligibility and tests**
- "24+ months of work experience (as of March 31st)" and a valid GMAT or GRE (test centre scores only). Source: https://www.isb.edu/en/study-isb/post-graduate-programmes/pgp-management.html
- ISB FAQ: "Part-time work experience will not be considered"; regular admissions need at least two years full-time. "There is no specific minimum score required for the GMAT/GRE." Scores valid 5 years. Online/home-based scores not accepted from the April 2025 intake. Source: https://isb.edu/programmes/post-graduate-programmes/pgp-in-management/faqs
- ISB admits ~820 across both campuses per year; Class of 2025 had 816 enrolled. Source: https://www.isb.edu/en/study-isb/post-graduate-programmes/pgp-management.html
- English test: "A TOEFL/IELTS score is required for non-Indian students who did not have English as the primary language of instruction." Source: ISB FAQ above.

**Class profile (Class of 2026, OFFICIAL placements page)**
| Metric | Figure | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Class size | 812 | https://placements.isb.edu/isbebook/?p=223 |
| Women | 47% | same |
| Avg work experience | "four years" | same |
| GMAT mean (mid 80%) | 683; range 635-730 (page does not say Classic vs Focus) | same |

- Third-party (GOALISB) says Class of 2026: GMAT Focus average 669 (range 555-765), Classic average 720 (640-780); 4.02 yrs experience; 47% women; class 826. UNVERIFIED: https://www.goalisb.com/post/gmat-score-for-isb (search-result text). Conflicts with ISB's 812. CrackVerbal reported 38% women and 4.2 yrs; discounted as conflicting with ISB page.

**Fees (INR)**

Collegedunia lists a different, higher range (INR 40.47-44.45 lakh for a PGPM listing), which conflicts: https://collegedunia.com/college/17905-indian-school-of-business-isb-mohali
- Application fee: base amount not stated on ISB FAQ; reapplicants get 50% waiver. UNVERIFIED third-party range INR 5,000-10,000: https://www.jamboreeindia.com/know-how/how-to-get-into-isb

**Rounds and deadlines**
- ISB official: "Each intake has three staggered deadlines. The online application for each round opens about two months before that round's deadline." Page text is internally inconsistent about whether Class of 2028 is open or "Round 1 for Class of 2027-28 will be announced soon." Source: https://www.isb.edu/en/study-isb/post-graduate-programmes/pgp-management.html
- UNVERIFIED (third-party): PGP Round 2 December 6, 2026; no applications after January 17, 2027. Source: https://matchtocollege.com/news-and-updates/isb-hyderabad-mohali-pgp-admissions-2027-28-deadlines-guide . Another site reports PGP Round 1 as September 20, 2026 (search-result text; not verified).
- Older ISB blog: two cycles for Indian passport holders, mid-September and early January (cycle year unclear). Source: search result for https://blogs.isb.edu/admissionsdirector/?p=395 and https://www.mba.com/explore-programs/choose-and-compare-programs/indian-school-of-business
- ISB FAQ: all three rounds have "the same eligibility criteria"; applying in any round "doesn't materially affect selection chances". Source: ISB FAQ above. Another page says earlier-round applicants often get preference in scholarship allocation (UNVERIFIED): matchtocollege link above.
- Expected 2027-28 timing (inference from the pattern, not a source): Round 1 ~mid/late Sept 2027, Round 2 ~early Dec 2027, Round 3 ~mid Jan 2028. UNVERIFIED.

**Application components and interview**
- Essays: "three compulsory and one optional essay for the first time applicants"; reapplicants add a compulsory Essay 4. Source: https://blogs.isb.edu/admissionsdirector/?p=395 (cycle year not stated).
- Recommendation: "We only need one LOR"; from someone who "closely observed your work in the recent past"; family and friends not accepted. Source: ISB FAQ above. Blog: "One evaluation from a professional contact, preferably a current supervisor." https://blogs.isb.edu/admissionsdirector/?p=395
- Interview: "Every shortlisted candidate is interviewed, and there is no group discussion." Overseas candidates by phone/Skype. Source: ISB FAQ above. Format/length of interview: not published (not found).
- Shortlisting is based on academics, GMAT/GRE, work experience, essays and supervisor evaluation (search-result text of ISB FAQ).

**Scholarships (ISB)**
- Official: scholarships span "nine categories" and support 25% of students each year; flexible loan options via partner banks. https://www.isb.edu/en/study-isb/post-graduate-programmes/pgp-management.html
- ISB FAQ: waivers of 25% to 100% available regardless of round; all applicants auto-considered for merit-based; need-based requires family income not above Rs 18 lakh. https://isb.edu/programmes/post-graduate-programmes/pgp-in-management/faqs
- Women-specific: "Prof. Manasa Mandava Memorial Scholarship", 50% of tuition, automatically considered, two 25% instalments. UNVERIFIED (aggregator): https://admitstreet.com/blog/isb-scholarships/
- Need-cum-merit 75% (family income above INR 8 lakh up to INR 20 lakh) and 100% (up to INR 8 lakh) figures conflict with the FAQ's Rs 18 lakh cap; UNVERIFIED.
