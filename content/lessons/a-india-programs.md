# India Programmes: ISB, SPJIMR, XLRI and Beyond

India has some of the best management schools in Asia, but the GMAT only opens a few of those doors. This lesson shows you which ones, what they ask for, and which are for your future self.

> [!NOTE]
> Facts here come from official pages where we could confirm them, and from third-party sites where we couldn't. We mark the second kind "confirm on the official site". Deadlines and fees change every cycle, so use this as a planning map, not a final answer.

## ISB PGP: your most likely Indian target

The Post Graduate Programme in Management at the Indian School of Business runs on two campuses, Hyderabad and Mohali. It admits roughly 820 students a year.

### Eligibility

ISB asks for 24+ months of full-time work experience as of 31 March. Part-time work isn't counted. You need a valid GMAT or GRE taken at a test centre (home-based scores aren't accepted from the April 2025 intake). There's no specific minimum score, and scores are valid for five years.

> [!REMEMBER]
> The 24 months is counted "as of 31 March". The ISB page doesn't make the reference year obvious. Before you plan around it, confirm on isb.edu which 31 March applies to the 2028 intake. If it's 31 March 2028, you'd have roughly 28 months.

For Indian citizens who studied in English, ISB says a TOEFL or IELTS is needed only for non-Indian students who didn't have English as the main language of instruction. You're covered, but keep a Medium of Instruction certificate handy (see the logistics lesson).

### Class profile (Class of 2026, from ISB's placement page)

| Metric | Figure |
|---|---|
| Class size | 812 |
| Women | 47% |
| Average experience | about four years |
| GMAT mean | 683 (middle 80%: 635 to 730) |

The page doesn't say whether those scores are Classic or Focus, so take them as a rough guide only. A third-party site reports different figures; we've set it aside because it conflicts with ISB's own page.

What this means for you: 47% women is encouraging. Four years average experience means you'll be one of the less experienced people in the room, so your leadership evidence and clarity of goals do the heavy lifting.

### Essays, recommendation and interview

- **Essays:** three compulsory and one optional for first-time applicants (reapplicants have an extra compulsory one). Exact prompts change each year, so read the current ones on ISB's site.
- **Recommendation:** one evaluation, preferably from a current supervisor, from someone who has closely observed your recent work. Family and friends aren't accepted.
- **Interview:** every shortlisted candidate is interviewed. There's no group discussion. Overseas candidates are interviewed remotely. ISB doesn't publish the length or format.
- **Shortlisting inputs:** academics, GMAT or GRE, work experience, essays and the supervisor's evaluation.

### Rounds

ISB says each intake has three staggered deadlines, and the application opens about two months before each deadline. Third-party sites suggest a pattern of roughly mid or late September, early December and mid-January, but those dates are inferred. For the 2028 intake, treat them as "approximately, confirm on isb.edu".

ISB's FAQ says applying in any round doesn't materially change your chances. Some advisers say earlier applicants get scholarship preference, but that's unconfirmed.

### Fees and scholarships

ISB's page states a uniform fee for all candidates but doesn't show the figure. Third-party sites say tuition is around INR 31 lakh plus GST, with the all-in cost (including accommodation) in the high 30s to low 40s of lakhs. Treat that as indicative only.

On scholarships, ISB says it supports about 25% of students across nine categories, with waivers from 25% to 100%. Every applicant is automatically considered for merit-based awards. Need-based help has a family income ceiling (the FAQ mentions Rs 18 lakh). A women-specific award, the Prof. Manasa Mandava Memorial Scholarship (50% of tuition), is reported by an aggregator but unconfirmed, so ask ISB directly.

> [!TIP]
> Money conversations are easier early. Write the funding section of your plan before you apply, not after you get in. The funding lesson walks through it.

## ISB PGP Young Leaders

ISB's early-career track, PGP Young Leaders, is listed for people with 0 to 2 years of experience. Its Class of 2029 cycle had Round 1 on 20 December 2026 and Round 2 on 15 February 2027. At your stage, you'd fall at or beyond the edge of the YL experience range by the time a 2028 cycle opens, and the programme schedule may differ. It's worth asking ISB which track suits you. Don't assume either way.

## SPJIMR

S.P. Jain Institute of Management and Research in Mumbai is respected for its social-impact and values-led approach. Its PGDM accepts CAT or GMAT, has no minimum experience, and takes freshers up to about five years. Third-party sources put the 2027-29 application deadline in late November 2026 and the fee around INR 24.9 lakh. We couldn't confirm any of this on SPJIMR's own site, and we didn't find a class profile. If a two-year PGDM appeals, check https://www.spjimr.org/ and weigh whether you want to apply for an immediate 2027 start. That would be before your GMAT is ready, so for you it's mostly a 2028 conversation.

Its PGPM for experienced professionals wants three years, which you'd reach around late 2028.

## XLRI PGDM-GM (General Management)

XLRI in Jamshedpur takes GMAT or GRE for its General Management stream only, and it asks for three years of managerial experience. Third-party sources suggest GMAT or GRE applications close around the end of December each year. Other XLRI streams are XAT-based. For you, GM is a 2029 conversation at the earliest. Confirm on XLRI's official site.

## Great Lakes PGPM

Great Lakes Institute of Management in Chennai runs a one-year PGPM aimed at around two years of experience. It accepts several tests, including the GMAT. Aggregators say it offers diversity and merit scholarships with a share reserved for women. None of this is confirmed officially, so treat it as a lead to investigate, not a fact.

## IIM one-year programmes: your future option

These are strong programmes, but they ask for much more experience than you have.

| Programme | Experience (as stated in research) |
|---|---|
| IIM Ahmedabad PGPX | 4 years |
| IIM Lucknow IPMX (Noida) | 4 years |
| IIM Bangalore EPGP | 5 years |
| IIM Calcutta PGPEX / MBAEx | 5 years |
| IIM Indore EPGP | 5 years (third-party) |

IIM Ahmedabad's most recent PGPX batch averaged about seven and a half years of experience, with 29% women. At IIM Calcutta, GMAT carries 25% of the selection weight, and at IIM Lucknow it's 30%. So a strong GMAT matters a lot there, and your 2027 score can still be valid (five years) when you apply around 2031.

> [!NOTE]
> We found no IIM Indore programme called "IPMX". Its one-year programme is EPGP. Don't rely on the name you hear in conversations; check each school's own page.

## What is not open to you

- **MDI Gurgaon:** its official page says the GMAT score for Indian candidates is not applicable. CAT is needed. The only GMAT exception we found, per a third party, is its International Business programme, which has a different degree-length condition. Don't count on it.
- **IIM two-year PGP via GMAT:** IIM Ahmedabad and IIM Indore accept it for foreign-passport holders. Indian citizens resident in India go through CAT instead.

This isn't a loss. It just means your GMAT prep points toward ISB, the Great Lakes and SPJIMR group, and the global programmes.

## Key takeaways
- ISB PGP is your main India target: 24+ months as of 31 March, test-centre GMAT or GRE, one recommendation, interview for everyone shortlisted.
- ISB's Class of 2026 had 47% women, about four years of average experience, and a GMAT mean of 683 (scale not specified).
- SPJIMR's PGDM accepts the GMAT with no minimum experience, and XLRI's GM stream needs three years of managerial experience.
- IIM one-year programmes want 4 to 5 years, so they're a 2030-31 option. A 2027 GMAT score can still be valid.
- MDI Gurgaon and the IIM two-year PGP don't take the GMAT from Indian residents.
- Confirm every date and fee on the official site; many figures here come from third parties.
