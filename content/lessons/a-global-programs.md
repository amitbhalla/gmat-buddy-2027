# Global Programmes: MiMs, Marketing Masters and MBAs

Outside India, the same question comes up again and again: how much experience does this school expect? Get that wrong and you'll write great essays for a programme that was never built for you. This lesson sorts the main options by how well they fit someone with about two years of marketing experience.

> [!NOTE]
> Every figure below is indicative. Where a number came from a third-party site rather than the school, we say so. Always confirm on the official website. Deadlines in particular repeat in pattern but shift by a few days each year.

## The big idea: three groups

1. **Pre-experience Masters in Management (MiM):** built for people with 0 to 2 years of experience. You're at the edge.
2. **Specialised Masters (marketing, strategy):** some flex up to about three years.
3. **Full-time MBAs:** built for 4 to 6 years. Mostly a later option, with a few exceptions.

Where you stand: the two-year caps at LBS MiM, Imperial and Kellogg are the issue. By September 2027 you'd have about 22 months. By March 2028 you'd have passed two years. So these programmes are "apply early or lose the window".

> [!TRAP]
> "About two years" isn't a safe answer. Some schools count to the start of the programme, some to the application date. A school that counts to the start date in August 2028 would see you at nearly three years. Read the fine print, or email admissions with your exact months and ask.

### Why MiMs still matter

Some are shorter than you'd think (Imperial's is 12 to 16 months, though several French programmes run two years), they give an international degree, and they feed strong recruiting pipelines in consulting, consumer goods and tech. The downside is cost, and that classmates are often 22 to 24 years old. You'd bring a year or two of real work, which is an advantage, as long as you're within the caps.

## Group 2: Marketing and specialised Masters

**Imperial MSc Strategic Marketing:** designed for new or recent graduates, but its official page says candidates with more than three years of experience are considered case by case. The 2027 entry fee is GBP 48,000. This is likely your best "marketing-first" fit among those we found, since the 2.3-year profile sits inside the stated flexibility.

**ESSEC MSc Marketing Management and Digital:** the listing we found could not be confirmed, and its campus and fee are unclear. Treat it as a lead to research.

> [!TIP]
> Specialised Masters are great if you already know you want a career in marketing leadership. They're less ideal if you want to switch function. A generalist programme (ISB or MBA) gives you more room to pivot.

## Group 3: Full-time MBAs

| Wharton MBA (Class of 2026) | Average 5 years | 866 students, 47% women |
| NUS MBA | Minimum 2 years full-time at application | Avg Focus about 615 (third-party) |
| NTU Nanyang MBA | Minimum 2 years | S$82,000 (2025 intake, official) |
| HKUST MBA | Minimum 2 years (third-party) | Avg GMAT around 660 (third-party) |

Notice the Asian MBAs. NUS, NTU and HKUST ask for two years minimum, which you'd have around late 2027. They're real options, though applications at a "bare minimum" experience level are rare. They're a good place for a partner school in a list, not your main bet.

### HBS 2+2 and similar deferred programmes

HBS has a 2+2 route for people in their final year of study who commit to work for two to four years, then join. It's not designed for someone with a year of full-time experience, and the official page wasn't available to us. Confirm directly if you're curious. For you, it's most likely a "no".

> [!NOTE]
> INSEAD, LBS and HBS at 4 to 6 years of experience are future options. The single most useful thing you can do now is get a strong GMAT and a clear, quantified career record, so that in 2030 you're ready.

## Choosing between the groups

Ask yourself three questions:

1. **What do I want my career to look like in five years?** A marketing specialist in a global brand? A strategist or general manager? Founder?
2. **Do I want to stay in India or move abroad?** European MiMs open the European job market (with visa limits). ISB opens India and increasingly global roles.
3. **How much debt am I willing to carry?** The programmes above range from roughly INR 30 lakh to well over INR 90 lakh all-in. We'll get into that in the funding lesson.

The concordance table from GMAC maps Classic to Focus scores by percentile. For example, a Classic 700 maps to a Focus score of roughly 645 to 655. If a school quotes Classic averages, convert before comparing.

## Key takeaways
- European and US MiMs cap experience near two years; you're at the edge, so early rounds matter.
- Imperial MSc Strategic Marketing is the most marketing-specific option and flexes up to about three years case by case.
- INSEAD, LBS MBA, HBS and Wharton average 4.9 to 5.5 years, so they're a 2030 conversation.
- NUS, NTU and HKUST ask for a minimum of two years and could work after late 2027.
- Compare Classic and Focus scores with the official concordance, not by eye.
- Tuition and deadlines here are indicative; confirm on each school's site.
