# Logistics: English Tests, Transcripts, Verification and Visas

None of this is glamorous, and all of it can derail a good application if you leave it late. The paperwork takes weeks, sometimes months. This lesson helps you get ahead of it quietly, in short bursts, long before deadlines.

> [!NOTE]
> Rules for documents, English tests and visas change often and differ by country and school. We give you the general picture and tell you what to ask. Treat any detail as "confirm on the official site" before you act, especially visa information, which only the relevant government can confirm.

## English tests: do you need TOEFL or IELTS?

Often, no. But the rule is different for each school, and the safest thing is a document that proves your English-medium education.

What the research tells us:

| School | What it said |
|---|---|
| ISB | A TOEFL or IELTS score is required for non-Indian students who didn't have English as the primary language of instruction. For Indian students educated in English, not required |
| HEC Paris MiM | English proof via TOEIC, TOEFL, IELTS or Cambridge; waived if your entire prior degree was taught in English, with a university statement |
| INSEAD | TOEFL, IELTS, PTE, Duolingo or an English-taught degree can qualify |
| LBS | Non-native English speakers usually submit a test unless it is waived at the school's discretion; applicants whose degree was taught in another language must prove fluency |

For you, the most useful single document is a **Medium of Instruction (MOI) certificate**.

### Getting a Medium of Instruction certificate

This is a letter from your university or college confirming that your degree was taught and examined in English.

1. Contact the office of Jesus and Mary College (the examination or administrative office) and ask whether they issue one. Many Delhi University colleges do, and some route it through the university.
2. If the college can't, ask the University of Delhi's relevant office. Procedures differ, so ask at the counter and in writing.
3. Request several originals. Some schools want a hard copy; many accept a scan.
4. Ask for it on letterhead, with a signature, stamp, your name, programme and years.

> [!TIP]
> Start this in the first half of 2027. Government and university offices can be slow, and you may need to visit in person. Our research hasn't confirmed whether a particular college issues this letter, so treat it as a request to make, not a guarantee.

> [!TRAP]
> Don't assume your degree "obviously" counts as English-medium. Some programmes at Indian universities offer options in Hindi, so schools ask for a statement. Get the letter anyway; it costs little and avoids a last-minute test.

## Transcripts

Most schools want transcripts (mark sheets) from your undergraduate degree. Sometimes unofficial copies are fine at the application stage and official ones are required after admission. Always read each school's rule.

Collect now:

- All semester or year mark sheets for your degree.
- Your degree certificate or provisional certificate.
- Your Class X and XII mark sheets (some schools ask).
- A clear scan of each, plus originals stored safely.

If you need official transcripts sent directly from the university, ask the examination branch about the process, cost and timing. Ask early, as it can take weeks.

## Degree verification and credential evaluation

Some countries and schools ask for independent evaluation of foreign degrees. A service like WES (World Education Services) is commonly used for this in the US and Canada. Other countries have their own bodies or rely on the school's own checks.

We haven't researched which schools on your list require this. Add one line to your tracker for each programme: "Does it require an external credential evaluation or verification?" and fill it in from the admissions page.

> [!REMEMBER]
> Employers and universities often run background checks. Make sure the dates and job titles on your resume match your official records exactly.

### Degree length

A point to confirm: some programmes expect a particular degree length. For example, a third-party source says MDI's international business programme needs a four-year bachelor's. Many Indian degrees are three years. For each programme, check whether a three-year bachelor's is accepted. Policies vary by school and country, so confirm for every school on your list whether a three-year bachelor's is accepted.

## Passport and identity

- Check your passport's validity. Many countries need six months or more beyond your stay, and you'll want pages free for visas.
- Make sure the name on your passport matches the name on your application, test registration and degree certificates. A mismatch can block test-day entry or a visa.
- If you don't have a passport, apply now. It's a simple task with a long, unpredictable wait.

## GMAT test-day identification

Your GMAT registration name must match your ID exactly. Book as soon as the booking window opens (about six months ahead, so around 28 February 2027 for your 28 August 2027 attempt), because weekend slots fill. Early July 2027 is the latest sensible date, about six to eight weeks ahead.

## Visas overview

You'll only need one after you get an offer, but it helps to know the shape of the process.

| Destination | Visa type | What to expect |
|---|---|---|
| UK | Student visa | Offer, confirmation of acceptance from the school, proof of funds, health surcharge, biometrics |
| France | Long-stay student visa | School admission, proof of funds, accommodation proof, consulate or agency process |
| USA | F-1 | School's I-20 form, visa interview, proof of funds |
| Singapore, Hong Kong | Student pass / student visa | School-led process |

We haven't verified any specific fee, amount or timeline. Check the official government immigration website for the country, and your school's international student page.

### Post-study work

Whether you can work after graduating differs by country and changes with policy. It affects your return on investment, so ask each school's career services what graduates from India typically do, and verify the current rules on official government pages.

### Timing

Plan on starting visa steps within a few days of accepting an offer, because proof of funds, accommodation and appointments take time. For a programme starting in autumn 2028, you'd likely begin in spring or early summer 2028.

## Key takeaways
- Many schools waive English tests if your degree was in English, but they want proof.
- Request a Medium of Instruction certificate from JMC or DU in the first half of 2027.
- Collect and scan transcripts and certificates early; official copies can take weeks.
- Add a line per school asking whether credential evaluation is required.
- Check passport validity and keep your name consistent across every document.
- Visa rules differ by country and change; always check official government sources.
