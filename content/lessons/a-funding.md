# Funding Your Degree: Costs, Scholarships and Loans

Money is the part of business school nobody likes talking about, and the part that most shapes your choices. The good news: there's real help available, especially for women, and a clear plan beats a vague hope. This lesson helps you work out what a programme really costs and how people pay for it.

> [!NOTE]
> Everything here is indicative. Fees, interest rates and scholarship amounts change often, and many figures in our research came from third-party sites that disagree with each other. Use this lesson to know what to ask and where to look, then confirm every number on the official page or with the lender.

## Step 1: Know the full cost

Tuition is only the beginning. For each programme on your list, add up:

| Cost | Notes |
|---|---|
| Tuition | The headline fee |
| Application fees | For example, LBS MiM GBP 125, INSEAD EUR 250, HEC MiM EUR 180 |
| Test fees | GMAT, plus an English test if needed |
| Living costs | Rent, food, transport, insurance |
| Travel and visa | Flights, visa fees, health surcharge where it applies |
| Lost income | Salary you'd earn if you kept working |
| One-time costs | Laptop, clothes for interviews, deposits |

> [!TRAP]
> Don't compare tuition alone. A cheaper tuition in an expensive city can cost more than a pricier programme in a cheaper one. And a one-year programme saves a year of lost income.

## Step 2: Scholarships you can actually target

### ISB

ISB says scholarships span nine categories, support about 25% of students each year, and waive between 25% and 100% of fees. Every applicant is automatically considered for merit-based awards. Need-based support has a family income ceiling (the FAQ mentions Rs 18 lakh). A third-party site says there's a women-specific memorial scholarship at 50% of tuition; we couldn't confirm it, so ask ISB. Some advisers say earlier rounds get more scholarship preference, also unconfirmed.

### INSEAD (for future MBA applications)

INSEAD offers a Women's Scholarship (EUR 18,000 to 25,000, several per class), and the MBA'81 Scholarship for Women (one per class, need-based, for Round 1 and 2 applicants). Other endowed awards for women exist, but we couldn't verify their terms. These are for the MBA, so they're relevant later, not in 2028.

### Forte Foundation fellowships

Forte fellowships are awarded through participating schools, usually with no separate application. Imperial's 2026-27 listing describes them for women with a leadership track record, commitment to the Forte mission, a strong and balanced GMAT or GRE, and professional references. Oxford's Saïd Business School lists up to GBP 30,000. We found no source excluding Indian nationals, but we couldn't confirm eligibility on Forte's own site, so check with each school.

### Great Lakes

Third-party reports say Great Lakes offers 40 diversity and merit scholarships of INR 5 lakh each for its one-year PGPM, with at least 30% reserved for women. Unconfirmed.

### LBS and HEC

We found no women-specific scholarship for LBS MiM or HEC MiM. LBS has a scholarship cut-off of 8 March in its MiM stages, so apply by then if you want to be considered.

> [!TIP]
> Make a scholarship calendar. Many deadlines come before the final application deadline, and some only consider the first two rounds.

## Step 3: Foundation and external support

**JN Tata Endowment for Higher Education:** an interest-free loan, up to about INR 20 lakh per aggregators, repayable over seven years, with a possible grant; Indian citizens, all disciplines. A typical timeline has screening in March to April, a test in May, interviews in May to June, and results in July. This is third-party information, so check the official site before relying on it.

**Inlaks Shivdasani:** significant awards (up to USD 100,000 per a third-party page) for Indian citizens under 30, with fewer than 35 awards a year and an emphasis on humanities, arts and applied fields. Whether MBA programmes are eligible isn't confirmed, and older notes mention a marks threshold. Research it, but don't plan on it.

## Step 4: Education loans (options to research)

Loans are how most people bridge the gap. Our research found these options. Rates conflict between sources, so these are indicative only.

| Lender | Indicative notes |
|---|---|
| SBI Global Ed-Vantage | Rates about 9 to 11% depending on collateral |
| HDFC Credila | Around 12 to 13%, up to INR 2 crore |
| Avanse | Around 10 to 16.5% |
| Prodigy Finance | Around 7 to 15% in USD, no Indian collateral, based on future earnings |
| ISB partner banks | ISB says flexible loan options exist through partner banks |

Collateral requirements at public banks tend to start above roughly INR 7.5 lakh. Interest may be eligible for a tax deduction under Section 80E; confirm with a tax adviser.

> [!REMEMBER]
> Always compare the total repayment, not only the interest rate. Look at moratorium (the period before repayments start), processing fees, prepayment charges and whether the rate is fixed or floating.

## Step 5: Build a budget

Create a simple three-line plan for each programme:

1. **Total cost** (tuition, living, travel, one-time).
2. **Funding sources:** savings, family support if any, scholarships (conservative guess), loans.
3. **Gap:** what's left. If you can't close it, the programme isn't realistic yet.

Then test: if the scholarship doesn't come, can I still afford this? If not, hold it as a reach.

### Return on investment, simply

Ask: what's my expected salary in the first year after the programme, and what will my monthly loan repayment be? As a guide, many advisers suggest keeping the repayment well within what you can pay comfortably. Don't assume placement numbers from marketing brochures apply to marketing roles; check the specific function.

Maria has two offers. A has tuition of INR 31 lakh with a 25% waiver and about INR 5 lakh living. B has tuition of GBP 51,000 (suppose INR 54 lakh) with no scholarship and INR 15 lakh living.

## Key takeaways
- Work out the total cost per programme, not just tuition, and round up.
- ISB supports about 25% of students through scholarships, with waivers from 25% to 100%.
- Forte fellowships are awarded through participating schools; confirm Indian eligibility with each.
- JN Tata, Inlaks and education loans are options to research, with third-party details to confirm.
- Compare loans by total repayment and terms, not only rate.
- Plan for the case where the scholarship doesn't arrive.
